63.4. The Customer's right to terminate does not extinguish the Customer's obligation to pay amounts that were due and payable prior to the effective date of termination, subject to the refund provisions of this Part 4.

63.5. The effective date of termination shall be:

(a) the date specified by the Customer in the termination request, provided it is not earlier than the date on which VIB receives the request; or

(b) where no date is specified, the date on which VIB receives the termination request.

**64. VIB Right to Terminate**

64.1. VIB may terminate this Agreement upon written or electronic notice to the Customer where:

(a) the Customer is in persistent default under Clause 55;

(b) the Customer has made a material false representation under this Agreement, including but not limited to false authority representations under Clause 20;

(c) the Customer has knowingly provided false or fabricated information in breach of Clause 17;

(d) continued performance of the Service is rendered impossible or impracticable due to circumstances beyond VIB's reasonable control, including third-party platform actions;

(e) the Customer has materially breached a term of this Agreement and has failed to cure the breach within a reasonable cure period after receiving notice; or

(f) termination is required by applicable law or by a lawful order of a competent authority.

64.2. Before exercising the right to terminate under Clause 64.1(a), (b), (c) or (e), VIB shall provide the Customer with reasonable notice and, where the breach is capable of cure, a reasonable opportunity to cure the breach.

**66. Termination for Persistent Default**

66.1. Where a persistent default has occurred under Clause 55, VIB may terminate this Agreement in accordance with Clause 64.1(a), subject to the final notice requirement in Clause 55.3.

66.2. Termination for persistent default does not extinguish the Customer's obligation to pay outstanding amounts that were due and payable prior to termination, subject to the refund provisions of this Part 4 and applicable law.

66.3. VIB shall not treat termination for persistent default as an automatic forfeiture of all payments made by the Customer. The treatment of payments already made is governed by the refund provisions in Section Y of this Part 4.

**67. Effect of Termination**

67.1. Upon the effective date of termination:

(a) VIB's obligation to perform the Service ceases, except to the extent required by the surviving provisions of this Agreement;

(b) the Customer's obligation to make future instalment payments ceases, except for amounts that were due and payable prior to the effective date of termination;

(c) the provisions of this Agreement that are expressly stated to survive termination, or that by their nature are intended to survive termination, shall continue in effect;

(d) the Parties shall comply with their respective post-termination obligations as set out in Section Z of this Part 4; and

(e) accrued rights and obligations of the Parties as at the effective date of termination are preserved.

67.2. Termination does not affect:

(a) any right or obligation that has accrued prior to the effective date of termination;

(b) the operation of any provision that is expressly or by implication intended to survive termination; or

(c) any mandatory right of either Party under applicable law.

### SECTION Y — REFUNDS

**72. General Refund Principles**

72.1. The following principles apply to all refunds under this Agreement:

(a) the Customer shall not automatically forfeit all payments made merely because the Agreement is terminated, cancelled or not completed, except where the entire Total Contract Price has been earned by VIB through complete performance of the Service;

(b) refunds shall be calculated on a fair and proportionate basis, taking into account the stage of Service completion, the work already performed, and the costs reasonably incurred by VIB;

(c) refunds shall be processed within a reasonable time after the effective date of termination or cancellation;

(d) refund calculations shall be documented and provided to the Customer; and

(e) refunds are subject to applicable law, including any mandatory refund rights or consumer protection provisions.

72.2. Where applicable law provides the Customer with refund rights that are more favourable than those set out in this Agreement, the applicable law shall prevail.

**73. Refund Upon Customer Termination Before Commencement**

73.1. Where the Customer terminates this Agreement before Commencement under Clause 63 or cancels under Clause 68, the Customer shall be entitled to a refund of amounts paid, less any reasonable and documented costs incurred by VIB prior to cancellation.

73.2. Deductible costs under Clause 73.1 may include:

(a) reasonable administrative processing costs;

(b) third-party costs already incurred and non-recoverable; and

(c) other costs expressly identified in the Transaction Schedule as non-refundable upon pre-Commencement cancellation.

73.3. The deductible costs under Clause 73.2 shall not exceed a reasonable proportion of the Total Contract Price and shall be documented.

74.1. Where the Customer terminates this Agreement after Commencement under Clause 63 or cancels under Clause 69, the Customer may be entitled to a proportionate refund, calculated on the basis of:

(a) the proportion of the Service that has been completed at the effective date of termination;

(b) the Total Contract Price;

(c) amounts already paid by the Customer; and

(d) reasonable and documented costs incurred by VIB up to the effective date of termination.

74.2. Where the Service has been substantially completed (as determined by the proportion of the Transaction Schedule deliverables that have been performed), VIB may retain the full Total Contract Price or the portion proportionate to the completed work.

74.3. Where the Service has not been substantially completed, the Customer shall be entitled to a refund of the proportionate amount representing the uncompleted portion of the Service, less reasonable and documented costs.

### SECTION AA — SURVIVAL

**82. Surviving Clauses**

82.1. The following provisions shall survive the termination or cancellation of this Agreement:

(a) definitions and interpretation (Clause 1);

(b) no implied services (Clause 6), to the extent relevant to post-termination disputes;

(c) false authority representation (Clause 20);

(d) payment obligations for amounts due prior to termination (Clauses 44, 67.1(b));

(e) payment records (Clauses 49–51);

(f) consequences of default, to the extent relating to pre-termination defaults (Clauses 54–55);

(g) refund provisions (Clauses 72–77);

(h) post-termination obligations (Clauses 78–81);

(i) record retention (Clause 51);

(j) dispute-resolution provisions (as set out in Part 5);

(k) limitation of liability provisions (as set out in Part 5); and

(l) governing law provisions (as set out in Part 6).

82.2. The survival of a provision under Clause 82.1 does not extend that provision beyond its express scope or purpose.

**83. Limitation on Survival**

83.1. The survival of clauses under Clause 82 does not create new rights or obligations that did not exist during the term of the Agreement. Surviving clauses operate solely to preserve rights, obligations and protections that, by their nature or express terms, are intended to continue after termination.

83.2. Where a surviving clause cross-references another clause that has not survived, the cross-reference shall be read in the context of the surviving clause's purpose and shall not be interpreted to revive the terminated clause.

**84. Post-Termination Dispute Resolution**

84.1. Disputes arising from or in connection with the termination, cancellation or post-termination obligations of this Agreement shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute-resolution provisions set out in Part 5 of this Agreement.

84.2. The Customer's mandatory rights under applicable law in relation to post-termination disputes are preserved.

84.3. The commencement of post-termination dispute-resolution proceedings does not, by itself, revive the Agreement or create an obligation on VIB to resume the Service, unless the outcome of the dispute-resolution process expressly requires it.
