### SECTION KK — FORCE MAJEURE

**131. Force Majeure Event**

131.1. A "Force Majeure Event" means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party that prevents or materially impedes the performance of that Party's obligations under this Agreement, including but not limited to:

(a) natural disasters (including earthquakes, floods, storms, hurricanes and volcanic eruptions);

(b) epidemics, pandemics or public health emergencies;

(c) war, armed conflict, terrorism or civil unrest;

(d) government actions, sanctions, embargoes, regulatory changes or legal prohibitions;

(e) strikes, lockouts or other industrial action (other than by the affected Party's own employees, unless the action is industry-wide);

(f) failure of telecommunications, internet infrastructure or essential utility services;

(g) cyberattacks, distributed denial-of-service attacks or other malicious digital disruptions;

(h) third-party platform outages, shutdowns or policy changes that are beyond the affected Party's control;

(i) supply-chain disruptions that are beyond the affected Party's control;

(j) acts of God; and

(k) any other event or circumstance that is beyond the affected Party's reasonable control and could not have been reasonably foreseen or prevented.

131.2. A Force Majeure Event does not include:

(a) financial difficulty, insolvency or inability to pay, unless caused by a qualifying Force Majeure Event;

(b) changes in market conditions or commercial circumstances;

(c) the affected Party's own negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of contract; or

(d) events that were reasonably foreseeable at the time of entering into the Agreement and could have been reasonably prevented or mitigated.

**132. Effect of Force Majeure**

132.1. Where a Party is prevented from or materially impeded in performing an obligation under this Agreement by a Force Majeure Event, that Party shall be relieved of that obligation to the extent and for the duration that performance is prevented or materially impeded, provided that:

(a) the affected Party notifies the other Party within a reasonable time of the occurrence of the Force Majeure Event;

(b) the affected Party provides details of the event and its anticipated impact on performance; and

(c) the affected Party takes reasonable steps to mitigate the effect of the Force Majeure Event.

132.2. The notification under Clause 132.1(a) shall include:

|---|---|
| **Date of Notification** | ____ / ____ / ______ |
| **Nature of Force Majeure Event** | __________________________ |
| **Affected Obligation(s)** | __________________________ |
| **Anticipated Duration** | __________________________ |
| **Mitigation Steps Taken** | __________________________ |

132.3. Force Majeure does not relieve the Customer of the obligation to pay amounts that were due and payable prior to the occurrence of the Force Majeure Event, unless this Agreement expressly provides otherwise.

**133. Prolonged Force Majeure**

133.1. Where a Force Majeure Event continues for a period exceeding:

(a) the period specified in the Transaction Schedule; or

(b) where the Transaction Schedule does not specify a period, **ninety (90) calendar days**,

either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing written or electronic notice to the other Party.

133.2. Where the Agreement is terminated under Clause 133.1, the applicable refund provisions of Part 4 shall apply, and neither Party shall be liable to the other for the termination, except for obligations that accrued prior to the Force Majeure Event.

**134. Mitigation**

134.1. Each Party shall take reasonable steps to mitigate the effect of a Force Majeure Event on its obligations under this Agreement.

134.2. Where alternative means of performance are reasonably available, the affected Party shall use reasonable efforts to perform its obligations through those alternative means.

134.3. Upon cessation of the Force Majeure Event, the affected Party shall resume performance of its obligations within a reasonable time.
