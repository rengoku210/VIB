### SECTION K — SERVICE COMMENCEMENT

**23. Commencement of Service**

23.1. Commencement of the Service is subject to the fulfilment of the following conditions, unless the Transaction Schedule or this Agreement expressly provides otherwise:

(a) the Customer has accepted the Agreement in accordance with Clause 12 or Clause 13;

(b) the Transaction Schedule has been completed and accepted;

(c) the initial payment (where applicable) has been received or confirmed by VIB;

(d) the Customer has provided the access, information and authorisations reasonably required under this Agreement;

(e) identity verification (where required under Clause 18) has been completed to VIB's reasonable satisfaction; and

(f) any conditions precedent recorded in the Transaction Schedule or Special Conditions have been satisfied or waived by VIB.

23.2. VIB shall notify the Customer of Commencement or, where applicable, of any delay in Commencement and the reason for such delay, within a reasonable time.

23.3. Where the Transaction Schedule specifies a particular Commencement date or timeframe, VIB shall use reasonable efforts to commence the Service within that date or timeframe, subject to the fulfilment of the conditions in Clause 23.1.

23.4. The Service shall not be treated as having commenced until the conditions in Clause 23.1 have been satisfied, unless VIB expressly confirms Commencement notwithstanding the non-fulfilment of one or more conditions.

**24. Delay in Commencement**

24.1. Where Commencement is delayed due to the Customer's failure to provide required access, information, authorisations or payments, VIB shall not be liable for the resulting delay.

24.2. Where Commencement is delayed due to circumstances within VIB's reasonable control, VIB shall notify the Customer within a reasonable time and shall use reasonable efforts to minimise the delay.

24.3. Where Commencement is delayed due to third-party dependencies (including, without limitation, third-party platform availability, third-party verification processes or third-party transfer requirements), VIB shall notify the Customer of the dependency and the anticipated impact, but shall not be liable for delays caused by the acts or omissions of third parties.

24.4. The rights and obligations of the Parties in the event of prolonged or unreasonable delay are governed by the applicable termination and refund provisions of this Agreement.

### SECTION L — CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

**25. Service Instructions**

25.1. The Customer shall provide VIB with clear, accurate and complete instructions reasonably necessary for VIB to perform the Service.

25.2. Service instructions may be provided in writing or through an authorised electronic mechanism, including the VIB platform, email or another mechanism agreed between the Parties.

25.3. Where the Customer's instructions are ambiguous, incomplete or contradictory, VIB may:

(a) request clarification before proceeding;

(b) proceed on the basis of a reasonable interpretation, provided VIB notifies the Customer of the interpretation adopted; or

(c) suspend the relevant aspect of the Service until clarification is received.

25.4. VIB shall not be liable for delays, errors or outcomes resulting from the Customer's failure to provide clear, accurate and complete instructions, provided VIB acted reasonably in the circumstances.

25.5. The Customer acknowledges that VIB is entitled to rely upon instructions received from the Customer or from a person reasonably believed by VIB to be authorised by the Customer, subject to applicable law.

**26. Customer Access**

26.1. Where the Service requires access to the Digital Profile, associated account environment, platform credentials, configuration settings or related digital resources, the Customer shall provide such access within a reasonable time of VIB's request.

26.2. The access provided by the Customer may include, as applicable:

(a) login credentials;

(b) authentication codes or tokens;

(c) profile administrative access;

(d) platform-specific permissions;

(e) verification codes; and

(f) other access reasonably required for the performance of the Service.

26.3. The Customer shall ensure that the access provided is sufficient for VIB to perform the Service. Where the access provided is insufficient, VIB may request additional access and the Service may be delayed until adequate access is provided.

26.4. VIB shall use access provided under this Clause 26 solely for the purpose of performing the Service and in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

26.5. The Customer's obligation to provide access does not transfer ownership or control of the Digital Profile to VIB, except to the limited extent necessary for the performance of the Service during the Service period.

**27. Temporary Access**

27.1. Where VIB requires temporary access to the Digital Profile or associated resources for the purpose of performing the Service, the following conditions apply:

(a) the temporary access is limited to the duration reasonably required to perform the applicable Service;

(b) the temporary access is used solely for the purpose of performing the Service;

(c) VIB shall not use temporary access for any purpose unrelated to the Service;

(d) VIB shall take reasonable measures to protect access credentials provided by the Customer during the period of temporary access; and

(e) upon completion of the applicable Service, or upon the Customer's reasonable request, VIB shall cease using the temporary access and shall take reasonable steps to remove, disable or return access credentials, as applicable.

27.2. The Customer may revoke temporary access at any time, subject to the understanding that revocation during the performance of the Service may result in delay, inability to complete the Service, or the need for the Service to be recommenced.

27.3. VIB shall not be liable for Service delays or incomplete performance resulting from the Customer's revocation of access during the performance of the Service.

**28. Continuing Customer Control**

28.1. Nothing in this Agreement transfers permanent ownership, permanent control or permanent administrative rights over the Digital Profile to VIB, unless the Transaction Schedule expressly provides for a transfer of the Digital Profile to VIB or to a third party as the subject of the Service.

28.2. The Customer retains, subject to this Agreement:

(a) the right to revoke access in accordance with Clause 27.2;

(b) the right to provide further instructions in accordance with Clause 25;

(c) the right to request information about the status of the Service; and

(d) such other rights as are expressly preserved by this Agreement or by applicable law.

28.3. VIB's temporary access and use of the Digital Profile during the performance of the Service does not create a proprietary interest, continuing entitlement or licence in favour of VIB, except to the extent expressly provided in the Transaction Schedule.

### SECTION M — SERVICE STANDARDS AND SCOPE

**29. Configuration Standards**

29.1. Where the Service includes Digital Profile Configuration, VIB shall perform the configuration with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with the specifications identified in the Transaction Schedule.

29.2. Configuration shall be performed in accordance with the applicable platform's technical requirements, constraints and available features as at the date of performance.

29.3. VIB does not guarantee that:

(a) a particular configuration outcome will be achievable on every platform;

(b) third-party platforms will not alter, reset or override configurations performed by VIB;

(c) configurations will be compatible with future platform updates;

(d) configurations will produce a specific competitive, financial or performance outcome for the Customer; or

(e) configurations will remain unaltered after the Service is completed.

29.4. Where the Transaction Schedule specifies particular configuration requirements, VIB shall use reasonable efforts to meet those requirements, subject to platform limitations and third-party constraints.

**30. Customisation**

30.1. Where the Service includes Profile Customisation, VIB shall perform customisation work in accordance with the scope identified in the Transaction Schedule.

30.2. The Customer acknowledges that customisation is limited to the features, options and capabilities available on the applicable platform at the time of performance.

30.3. Customisation does not automatically include:

(a) ongoing customisation after Service completion;

(b) re-customisation following platform updates or resets;

(c) customisation of features not available on the applicable platform; or

(d) customisation of third-party services, software or plugins not expressly included in the Transaction Schedule.

**31. Upgrades**

31.1. Where the Service includes Essential Profile Upgrades or other upgrade services, VIB shall perform the upgrades expressly included in the Transaction Schedule.

31.2. Upgrades not expressly included in the Transaction Schedule are not part of the Service and may require:

(a) a separate quotation;

(b) additional payment;

(c) additional time;

(d) a separate agreement or amendment; and/or

(e) confirmation that the requested upgrade is achievable on the applicable platform.

31.3. VIB does not guarantee that all desired upgrades will be achievable, as upgrades may depend on third-party platform availability, eligibility requirements, or conditions outside VIB's reasonable control.

**32. Preparation Services**

32.1. Where the Service includes Profile Preparation, Profile Readiness Services or Profile Organisation, VIB shall perform such services in accordance with the Transaction Schedule.

32.2. Preparation services may include, where expressly identified:

(a) organising existing profile information;

(b) preparing the Digital Profile for a subsequent service (such as transfer, migration or configuration); and

(c) verifying profile readiness against applicable requirements.

32.3. VIB does not represent that preparation services will resolve pre-existing defects, restrictions, limitations or pending processes associated with the Digital Profile, unless the Transaction Schedule expressly includes remediation of a specifically identified condition.

**33. Maintenance and Adjustment**

33.1. Where the Service includes Profile Maintenance or Profile Adjustment, VIB shall perform such services in accordance with the scope and duration specified in the Transaction Schedule.

33.2. Maintenance and adjustment services are limited to the scope expressly identified in the Transaction Schedule. Unless the Transaction Schedule provides otherwise, maintenance and adjustment does not include:

(a) indefinite or perpetual maintenance;

(b) maintenance of features or services not included in the original Service;

(c) maintenance required due to the Customer's own modifications to the Digital Profile after Service completion; or

(d) maintenance required due to third-party platform changes, updates or policy modifications.

33.3. Where the Transaction Schedule specifies a maintenance period, the maintenance obligation ceases at the end of that period unless extended by a subsequent agreement or amendment.

### SECTION P — CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE AND REVIEW

**39. Customer Acceptance**

39.1. Upon delivery of the Service, the Customer shall review the delivered Digital Profile and the delivery record within the review period specified in Clause 40.

39.2. The Customer confirms acceptance by acknowledging the following:

| | Acknowledgement |
|---|---|
| ☐ | I HAVE REVIEWED THE DELIVERED DIGITAL PROFILE. |
| ☐ | I HAVE REVIEWED THE DELIVERY RECORD. |
| ☐ | I ACCEPT THE SERVICE AS DELIVERED, SUBJECT TO MY RIGHTS UNDER THIS AGREEMENT AND APPLICABLE LAW. |

39.3. Where the Customer does not raise a deficiency notification within the review period specified in Clause 40, the Service shall be deemed accepted by the Customer, subject to:

(a) the Customer's rights under the defect review provisions of this Agreement; and

(b) any mandatory rights under applicable law.

39.4. Deemed acceptance under Clause 39.3 does not extinguish the Customer's rights under applicable law in relation to defective services or services that do not conform to the Agreement.

**40. Review Period**

40.1. The review period for the Customer to inspect the delivered Service and raise any deficiency notification shall be:

(a) the period specified in the Transaction Schedule; or

(b) where the Transaction Schedule does not specify a review period, **forty-eight (48) hours** from the date and time of delivery notification.

40.2. During the review period, the Customer shall inspect the delivered Digital Profile and verify that the Service has been performed in accordance with the Transaction Schedule.

40.3. Where the Customer identifies a deficiency during the review period, the Customer shall notify VIB in writing or through an authorised electronic mechanism, providing:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date of Notification** | ____ / ____ / ______ |
| **Description of Deficiency** | __________________________ |
| **Affected Aspect of Service** | __________________________ |
| **Supporting Evidence (if available)** | __________________________ |

40.4. VIB shall acknowledge receipt of the deficiency notification within a reasonable time.

**41. Service Defect Review**

41.1. Upon receipt of a deficiency notification under Clause 40.3, VIB shall conduct a reasonable review of the reported deficiency.

41.2. Following the review, VIB may:

(a) confirm the deficiency and proceed to correction in accordance with Clause 42;

(b) confirm a partial deficiency and propose a corrective scope;

(c) determine that no deficiency exists and provide reasons; or

(d) request additional information from the Customer to complete the review.

41.3. VIB shall communicate the outcome of the defect review to the Customer within a reasonable time.

41.4. Where VIB and the Customer disagree on whether a deficiency exists, the dispute shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute-resolution provisions of this Agreement.

41.5. The defect review process does not create an obligation for VIB to provide unlimited re-performance. The scope and duration of correction are subject to the terms of this Agreement and the Transaction Schedule.

**42. Correction**

42.1. Where VIB confirms a deficiency under Clause 41.2(a) or 41.2(b), VIB shall use reasonable efforts to correct the deficiency within a reasonable time.

42.2. Correction shall be performed at no additional charge to the Customer where the deficiency results from VIB's failure to perform the Service in accordance with the Transaction Schedule.

42.3. Correction does not include:

(a) correction of deficiencies caused by the Customer's own actions or modifications after delivery;

(b) correction of deficiencies caused by third-party platform changes, updates, resets or policy modifications after delivery;

(c) services or deliverables not included in the original Transaction Schedule; or

(d) re-performance of the entire Service where the deficiency relates to a limited aspect of the Service.

42.4. Where correction is not reasonably achievable (including due to third-party platform limitations), VIB shall notify the Customer and the Parties shall discuss available alternatives, which may include partial correction, modification of the Service outcome, or the application of the applicable refund provisions of this Agreement.

42.5. Nothing in this Clause 42 limits the Customer's mandatory rights under applicable law in relation to defective or non-conforming services.
