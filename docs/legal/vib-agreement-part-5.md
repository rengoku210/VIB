(a) indirect, incidental or consequential loss or damage;

(b) loss of profits, revenue, business opportunity or anticipated savings;

(d) punitive, exemplary or aggravated damages, except as required by applicable law;

(e) loss or damage arising from the acts, omissions, decisions or policies of third-party platforms; or

(f) loss or damage arising from circumstances beyond VIB's reasonable control.

90.2. Subject to Clause 93, VIB's total aggregate liability to the Customer under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), statute or otherwise, shall not exceed:

(a) the amount specified in the Transaction Schedule as the liability cap; or

(b) where the Transaction Schedule does not specify a liability cap, the Total Contract Price actually paid by the Customer under this Agreement.

90.3. The limitation in Clause 90.2 applies to VIB's total aggregate liability and is not a per-incident cap, unless the Transaction Schedule expressly provides otherwise.

**91. Customer Limitation of Liability**

91.1. The Customer shall not be liable to VIB for:

(a) loss or damage arising from the acts, omissions, decisions or policies of third-party platforms;

(b) loss or damage arising from pre-existing conditions of the Digital Profile that were disclosed under Clause 21;

(c) loss or damage arising from circumstances beyond the Customer's reasonable control; or

(d) indirect, incidental or consequential loss or damage suffered by VIB, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

91.2. Nothing in Clause 91.1 limits the Customer's obligation to pay the Total Contract Price in accordance with this Agreement, or the Customer's liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or knowing breach of the Customer's representations.

**92. Mutual Limitation Principles**

92.1. The following principles apply to the limitation of liability under this Section CC:

(a) limitations of liability are intended to allocate risk fairly between the Parties and are not intended to exclude liability for deliberate wrongdoing;

(b) each Party shall take reasonable steps to mitigate loss or damage suffered in connection with this Agreement;

(c) where a Party contributes to its own loss through its own acts or omissions, the other Party's liability shall be reduced proportionately, to the extent permitted by applicable law; and

(d) the Parties acknowledge that the limitations in this Section CC were agreed as part of the overall commercial bargain and are reflected in the Total Contract Price.

**93. Exclusions from Limitation**

93.1. Nothing in this Section CC excludes or limits liability:

(a) for fraud, fraudulent misrepresentation or wilful misconduct;

(b) for death or personal injury caused by negligence, to the extent required by applicable law;

(c) that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law, including mandatory statutory liability; or

(d) under any indemnification obligation in Section DD that expressly provides for liability beyond the general cap.

**94. Mandatory Law Override**

94.1. Where applicable law prohibits the exclusion or limitation of liability for certain categories of loss, damage or breach, the applicable law shall prevail over the limitations in this Section CC.

94.2. The limitations in this Section CC are drafted to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law and shall be read down or modified to the minimum extent necessary to comply with applicable law, rather than being treated as void in their entirety.

94.3. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to exclude or limit consumer protection rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

### SECTION DD — INDEMNIFICATION

**95. Customer Indemnification of VIB**

95.1. The Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless VIB and its officers, employees, agents and representatives from and against any loss, damage, liability, cost or expense (including reasonable legal costs) arising from or in connection with:

(a) the Customer's false representation of authority over the Digital Profile in breach of Clause 19 or Clause 20;

(b) the Customer's provision of knowingly false, fabricated or materially misleading information in breach of Clause 17;

(c) the Customer's instructions to VIB that result in VIB acting upon unauthorised, unlawful or fraudulent instructions; or

(d) any third-party claim arising from the Customer's breach of intellectual property rights, ownership rights or authority requirements in connection with the Digital Profile.

95.2. The indemnification obligations in Clause 95.1 are subject to the indemnification procedure in Clause 97 and the limitations in Clause 98.

**96. VIB Indemnification of Customer**

96.1. VIB shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Customer from and against any loss, damage, liability, cost or expense (including reasonable legal costs) arising from or in connection with:

(a) VIB's negligent performance of the Service that directly causes loss or damage to the Customer's Digital Profile;

(b) VIB's material breach of its obligations under this Agreement that directly causes loss or damage to the Customer; or

(c) VIB's unauthorised use of the Customer's credentials, access or data for purposes outside the scope of the Service.

96.2. The indemnification obligations in Clause 96.1 are subject to the indemnification procedure in Clause 97, the limitations in Clause 98, and the general limitation of liability in Section CC.

**97. Indemnification Procedure**

97.1. A Party seeking indemnification (the "Indemnified Party") shall:

(a) promptly notify the other Party (the "Indemnifying Party") of the claim, loss or liability giving rise to the indemnification obligation;

(b) provide reasonable cooperation and assistance to the Indemnifying Party in the investigation and defence of the claim;

(c) permit the Indemnifying Party a reasonable opportunity to participate in the defence or resolution of the claim;

(d) not make any admission, settlement or compromise in relation to the claim without the Indemnifying Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld; and

(e) take reasonable steps to mitigate the loss or damage.

97.2. Failure to provide prompt notification under Clause 97.1(a) shall not extinguish the indemnification obligation, but may reduce the indemnification to the extent that the Indemnifying Party has been prejudiced by the delay.

**98. Limitation on Indemnification**

98.1. Indemnification under this Section DD shall be:

(a) proportionate to the actual loss or damage suffered;

(b) not punitive or exemplary in nature;

(c) subject to the aggregate liability cap in Clause 90.2, except where the claim arises from fraud, wilful misconduct or a matter excluded from limitation under Clause 93; and

(d) subject to applicable law.

98.2. Where an indemnification claim overlaps with a claim under the general limitation of liability provisions, the total recovery shall not exceed the greater of the two applicable limits, and the Indemnified Party shall not recover the same loss twice.

**99. Survival of Indemnification**

99.1. The indemnification obligations in this Section DD shall survive the termination or cancellation of this Agreement, in accordance with Clause 82, to the extent that the relevant claim, loss or liability arose from events occurring during the term of the Agreement.

### SECTION EE — FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION

**100. Prohibition of Fraud**

100.1. Each Party shall refrain from engaging in fraudulent conduct in connection with this Agreement.

100.2. Without limiting the generality of Clause 100.1, the following activities are prohibited:

(a) knowingly providing false, fabricated or materially misleading information for the purpose of obtaining a service, payment, refund or other benefit under this Agreement;

(b) knowingly misrepresenting authority, identity, capacity or ownership in connection with the Digital Profile;

(c) knowingly instructing VIB to perform services on a Digital Profile to which the Customer does not have lawful authority;

(d) knowingly using the Service to facilitate unauthorised access to, or misappropriation of, another person's digital profile, account or property; and

(e) knowingly manipulating transaction records, payment records or service records for the purpose of obtaining an unjust advantage.

100.3. The prohibitions in Clause 100.2 apply to both Parties. VIB is equally prohibited from engaging in fraudulent conduct in relation to the Customer's payments, data, credentials or Digital Profile.

**101. Consequences of Fraud**

101.1. Where VIB reasonably determines that the Customer has engaged in fraudulent conduct under Clause 100, VIB may, subject to applicable law:

(a) immediately suspend the Service;

(b) terminate the Agreement under Clause 64;

(c) withhold any pending refund, subject to the refund provisions of this Agreement and applicable law;

(d) preserve relevant records for the purpose of any investigation, legal proceedings or regulatory referral;

(e) report the matter to the appropriate law-enforcement or regulatory authorities, where required or permitted by applicable law; and

(f) pursue recovery of any loss or damage suffered by VIB as a result of the fraud through lawful means.

101.2. The exercise of any right under Clause 101.1 must be based on a reasonable determination of fraud, not merely on suspicion or disagreement. VIB shall not invoke Clause 101 in bad faith or as a pretext for avoiding legitimate service obligations.

101.3. Nothing in this Clause 101 compels the Customer to incriminate themselves. The Customer's mandatory rights under applicable law, including any rights in connection with criminal or regulatory investigations, are preserved.

(e) exercise any other rights available under applicable law.

104.2. VIB's liability for fraud is not subject to the general limitation of liability in Clause 90, in accordance with Clause 93.1(a).

### SECTION FF — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**105. Good Faith Negotiation**

105.1. Where a dispute, controversy or claim arises out of or in connection with this Agreement (a "Dispute"), the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the Dispute through good faith negotiation.

105.2. The Party raising the Dispute shall provide written or electronic notice to the other Party, setting out:

(a) the nature of the Dispute;

(b) the relevant facts and circumstances;

(c) the provision(s) of the Agreement that the Party believes are relevant; and

(d) the resolution sought.

105.3. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith for a period of **thirty (30) calendar days** from the date of the Dispute notice (the "Negotiation Period"), or such longer period as the Parties may agree.

105.4. Where the Dispute is not resolved within the Negotiation Period, either Party may escalate the Dispute to a senior representative of each Party for a further attempt at resolution before proceeding to the formal mechanisms in Clauses 107–109.

**106. Formal Complaint Mechanism**

107.1. Where the Dispute has not been resolved through negotiation under Clause 105 or through the formal complaint mechanism under Clause 106, either Party may propose mediation.

107.2. Mediation is voluntary unless the Transaction Schedule expressly requires mediation as a mandatory pre-condition to arbitration or court proceedings.

108.6. Nothing in this Clause 108 prevents either Party from seeking interim or urgent relief from a court of competent jurisdiction pending the outcome of the arbitration.

109.1. Where the Dispute is not subject to binding arbitration under Clause 108, either Party may commence court proceedings in the courts of the jurisdiction specified in the governing law provisions of Part 6 of this Agreement.

(c) the commencement of dispute-resolution proceedings does not suspend any applicable limitation period, unless applicable law provides otherwise.

110.2. The Parties shall continue to perform their respective obligations under this Agreement in good faith during the pendency of any Dispute, to the extent reasonably practicable.

### SECTION GG — CONFIDENTIALITY

**111. Confidential Information**

111.1. "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with this Agreement that:

(a) is marked, designated or identified as confidential;

(b) is by its nature reasonably understood to be confidential;

(c) includes personal information, financial information, account credentials or business information; or

(d) is identified as confidential in the Transaction Schedule.

111.2. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

(a) Customer account credentials, login information and verification codes;

(b) Customer personal and financial information;

(c) Digital Profile information and configuration;

(d) VIB's proprietary methods, processes and business information;

(e) transaction terms, pricing and commercial arrangements; and

(f) dispute-resolution communications and outcomes.

111.3. Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;

(b) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by contemporaneous records;

(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or

(d) is lawfully disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party without restriction.

**112. Obligations of Confidentiality**

112.1. The Receiving Party shall:

(a) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except as permitted under Clause 113;

(b) use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing obligations or exercising rights under this Agreement; and

(c) take reasonable security measures to protect Confidential Information from unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

112.2. The standard of care required under Clause 112.1(c) shall be no less than the standard the Receiving Party applies to its own confidential information of a similar nature.

**113. Permitted Disclosures**

113.1. A Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information:

(a) where required by applicable law, regulation, court order or lawful order of a competent authority;

(b) to its professional advisers (including legal, financial and accounting advisers) who are bound by professional obligations of confidentiality;

(c) to its employees, agents or contractors who need access to the information for the purpose of performing obligations under this Agreement, provided those persons are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Section GG;

(d) in connection with dispute-resolution proceedings under Section FF, to the extent reasonably necessary; or

(e) with the prior written or electronic consent of the Disclosing Party.

113.2. Where disclosure is required under Clause 113.1(a), the Receiving Party shall, to the extent permitted by applicable law, provide advance notice to the Disclosing Party.

**114. Duration of Confidentiality**

114.1. The confidentiality obligations in this Section GG shall continue for the duration of the Agreement and for a period of **two (2) years** following the effective date of termination or cancellation, or for such longer period as may be specified in the Transaction Schedule or required by applicable law.

114.2. Confidentiality obligations relating to Customer account credentials, personal information and financial information shall continue for as long as the information remains confidential in nature, subject to applicable law.

**115. Return or Destruction of Confidential Information**

115.1. Upon termination or cancellation of this Agreement, each Party shall, upon the other Party's reasonable request, return or destroy Confidential Information in its possession within a reasonable time.

115.2. A Party may retain copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law or for legitimate record-retention purposes, subject to the continuing confidentiality obligations in this Section GG.
