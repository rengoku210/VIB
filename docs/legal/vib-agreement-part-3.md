### SECTION T — LATE PAYMENT AND DEFAULT

**52. Late Payment**

52.1. Where the Customer fails to make a payment by the due date recorded in the instalment schedule, the payment shall be considered late from the day following the due date.

52.2. VIB shall provide the Customer with a late-payment notification within a reasonable time after a payment becomes overdue. The notification shall identify:

(a) the overdue instalment;

(b) the amount due;

(c) the original due date; and

(d) the applicable grace period, if any.

52.3. **Grace Period:** The Customer shall have a grace period of:

(a) the period specified in the Transaction Schedule; or

(b) where the Transaction Schedule does not specify a grace period, **seven (7) calendar days** from the original due date,

within which to make the overdue payment without the application of late-payment fees.

52.4. **Late-Payment Fee:**

|---|---|
| **Late-Payment Fee (if applicable)** | ₹ __________ or __________% of the overdue amount |
| **Fee Application** | Per occurrence / Per day / Per week / Other: __________ |
| **Maximum Late-Payment Fee Per Instalment** | ₹ __________________________ |

52.5. Late-payment fees, if applicable, shall be disclosed in the Agreement or Transaction Schedule prior to acceptance. VIB shall not impose late-payment fees that were not disclosed prior to acceptance, except as required by applicable law.

52.6. VIB shall notify the Customer before applying a late-payment fee to an overdue instalment.

52.7. Late-payment fees shall not be punitive or disproportionate. Where applicable law limits the amount or nature of late-payment fees, the applicable legal limit shall prevail.

**53. Payment Default**

53.1. A payment default occurs where:

(a) the Customer fails to make a payment within the grace period specified in Clause 52.3;

(b) the Customer fails to make two (2) or more consecutive instalments by their respective due dates; or

(c) the Customer communicates an express refusal to make further payments under the Agreement.

53.2. Upon the occurrence of a payment default, VIB shall issue a default notice to the Customer. The default notice shall include:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Default Notice Date** | ____ / ____ / ______ |
| **Nature of Default** | __________________________ |
| **Outstanding Amount** | ₹ __________________________ |
| **Cure Period** | __________________________ |
| **Consequences if Default is Not Cured** | __________________________ |

53.3. **Cure Period:** The Customer shall have a cure period of:

(a) the period specified in the Transaction Schedule; or

(b) where the Transaction Schedule does not specify a cure period, **fourteen (14) calendar days** from the date of the default notice,

within which to cure the default by making the outstanding payment(s) and any applicable late-payment fees.

53.4. Where the Customer cures the default within the cure period, the Agreement shall continue in full force and effect, and the default shall be treated as remedied.

**54. Consequences of Default**

54.1. Where the Customer fails to cure the default within the cure period specified in Clause 53.3, VIB may, subject to applicable law and the terms of this Agreement:

(a) suspend the Service in accordance with Clause 56;

(b) withhold delivery of uncompleted aspects of the Service;

(c) declare the full outstanding balance immediately due and payable;

(d) apply applicable late-payment fees in accordance with Clause 52;

(e) terminate the Agreement in accordance with the termination provisions of this Agreement; and/or

(f) pursue recovery of outstanding amounts through lawful means.

54.2. The exercise of any right under Clause 54.1 must be reasonable and proportionate in the circumstances. VIB shall not exercise multiple consequences simultaneously where a single consequence would be sufficient to protect VIB's legitimate interests.

54.3. VIB shall not automatically forfeit or retain payments already made by the Customer without applying the applicable refund and termination provisions of this Agreement. The treatment of payments already made in the event of default or termination is governed by the refund provisions in Part 4 of this Agreement.

54.4. The Customer's mandatory rights under applicable law are preserved notwithstanding any default. Nothing in this Clause 54 permits VIB to impose consequences that are prohibited or restricted by applicable law.

**55. Persistent Default**

55.1. Where the Customer remains in default after the expiry of two (2) consecutive cure periods, or where the total outstanding amount exceeds the threshold specified in the Transaction Schedule (if any), the default shall be treated as persistent default.

55.2. In the event of persistent default, VIB may, in addition to the measures available under Clause 54:

(a) treat the Agreement as repudiated by the Customer;

(b) cease all further Service performance;

(c) retain relevant records for the purpose of pursuing recovery through lawful means; and

(d) refer the matter for collection or legal proceedings, subject to applicable law.

55.3. Before exercising any right under Clause 55.2, VIB shall provide the Customer with a final formal written notice identifying:

(a) the persistent default;

(b) the total outstanding amount;

(c) the measures VIB intends to take; and

(d) a final opportunity for the Customer to cure the default within a reasonable period (which shall not be less than seven (7) calendar days from the date of the final notice).

55.4. The rights and remedies available to VIB under this Clause 55 are subject to applicable law, including any mandatory pre-litigation notice requirements, consumer protection requirements, and procedural fairness requirements.

56.3. Where the Customer cures the payment default during the suspension period, VIB shall resume the Service within a reasonable time, subject to the resumption conditions in Clause 59.

56.4. VIB shall not permanently delete, destroy or irreversibly alter the Customer's Digital Profile or associated data during a suspension period without the Customer's express consent, except as required by applicable law or by the applicable third-party platform's policies.

56.5. The duration of the suspension shall not exceed the period reasonably necessary to protect VIB's legitimate interests. Prolonged suspension may trigger the termination provisions of this Agreement.

**57. Service Holds**

57.1. VIB may place the Service on hold for reasons other than non-payment, including:

(a) pending verification of the Customer's identity or authority under Clause 18 or Clause 19;

(b) pending resolution of a suspected false authority representation under Clause 20;

(c) pending the Customer's provision of required access, information or instructions;

(d) pending the resolution of a third-party dependency that prevents further performance; or

(e) pending resolution of a safety, security or compliance concern, subject to applicable law.

57.2. Where VIB places the Service on hold, VIB shall notify the Customer of the hold, the reason for the hold, and the estimated duration, within a reasonable time.

57.3. The Customer may request information about the status of a service hold at any reasonable time, and VIB shall respond within a reasonable time.

**58. Effect of Suspension or Hold on Obligations**

58.1. During a suspension or hold period:

(a) the Customer's payment obligations continue to apply unless the Agreement or the applicable termination and refund provisions expressly provide otherwise;

(b) VIB's obligation to perform the Service is suspended to the extent affected by the suspension or hold;

(c) service timelines and delivery dates are adjusted to account for the duration of the suspension or hold; and

(d) VIB shall not accrue additional service obligations for the period during which the Service is suspended or held, unless the Transaction Schedule expressly provides otherwise.

58.2. The adjustment of timelines under Clause 58.1(c) does not create a right for VIB to indefinitely delay the Service after the suspension or hold is lifted.

**59. Resumption of Service**

59.1. The Service shall resume upon the satisfaction of the following conditions:

(a) the cause of the suspension or hold has been resolved;

(b) any outstanding payments required for resumption have been made;

(c) the Customer has provided any additional access, information or authorisations required for resumption; and

(d) VIB has confirmed that resumption is feasible, taking into account any changes to the Digital Profile or third-party platform that may have occurred during the suspension or hold period.

59.2. Upon resumption, VIB shall provide the Customer with a resumption confirmation:

### SECTION V — PAYMENT DISPUTES

60.1. The Customer may dispute a payment, charge or fee under this Agreement in good faith where the Customer reasonably believes that:

(a) the amount charged is incorrect;

(b) a payment was not properly credited;

(c) a fee was applied that was not disclosed prior to acceptance;

(d) a charge does not correspond to the agreed terms; or

(e) a payment was made but not acknowledged.

60.2. The Customer shall notify VIB of the payment dispute in writing or through an authorised electronic mechanism, providing:

|---|---|

| **Disputed Amount** | ₹ __________________________ |
| **Reason for Dispute** | __________________________ |
| **Supporting Evidence** | __________________________ |
| **Requested Resolution** | __________________________ |

60.3. Where a payment is disputed under this Clause 60, the undisputed portion of any outstanding amount remains payable in accordance with the Agreement.

**61. VIB Review of Payment Disputes**

61.1. Upon receipt of a payment dispute notification, VIB shall conduct a reasonable review of the dispute within a reasonable time.

61.2. Following the review, VIB may:

(a) confirm the Customer's dispute and issue a correction, credit or refund as applicable;

(b) confirm a partial error and propose a partial correction;

(c) determine that no error exists and provide reasons to the Customer; or

(d) request additional information from the Customer to complete the review.

61.3. VIB shall communicate the outcome of the payment dispute review to the Customer within a reasonable time.

**62. Escalation of Payment Disputes**

62.1. Where the Customer is not satisfied with the outcome of VIB's review under Clause 61, or where VIB fails to respond within a reasonable time, the Customer may escalate the dispute in accordance with the dispute-resolution provisions of this Agreement.

62.2. The Customer's mandatory rights under applicable law in relation to disputed charges, overcharges or unauthorised charges are preserved. Nothing in this Section V limits those rights.

62.3. The internal payment dispute process under Clauses 60–62 does not constitute a waiver of the Customer's right to pursue the dispute through the formal dispute-resolution mechanisms provided in this Agreement or under applicable law.
